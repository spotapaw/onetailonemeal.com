Earner app

Snap‑a‑Paw

Turn your pet into a work of art.

In development The portrait studio is built inside Care-a-Paw. It becomes its own app next.

Vision

Every pet gets its moment as art, and every portrait helps a street animal eat.

Mission

Beautiful AI portraits of pets in seconds, as images people share and prints they keep, with a share of every sale funding meals for street animals.

Why it exists

Impulse and gift income: fast to try, easy to share, so it also brings new people into the a-Paw family.

Who it’s for: Pet owners who love sharing their pet, people buying gifts, and people remembering a pet that has died.

What it does

Built around real days on the street and at home

  • Upload 1 to 3 photos, auto crop to the pet
  • Styles: oil, watercolour, royal, cartoon, pop art, seasonal
  • Memorial style, gentle wording, for pets that have passed
  • Save, share, set as wallpaper
  • Order a print (opens website shop)
  • Moderation: pets only, no people or unsafe images
Technology and AI

Where AI helps in Snap‑a‑Paw

Every line says whether it works today, is coming soon, or is planned.

  • Coming soonTurns a pet photo into a portrait: oil, watercolour, royal, cartoon, pop art and a gentle memorial style.
  • Coming soonSafety checks on every upload: pets only, nothing unsafe, and people are never painted.
Free

What everyone gets

  • 1 free portrait (with small logo)
  • Browse all styles
Paid

What costs money

  • Packs: 5 portraits A$4.99, 15 portraits A$11.99
  • Premium styles (royal, oil, seasonal) inside packs
  • Prints: canvas, framed print, mug (via print-on-demand, sold on the website)
  • Gift cards (website)
  • Care-a-Paw Premium includes 3 portraits a month

Prices are planned and may change before launch.

How it helps street animals

10% of Snap-a-Paw's net revenue funds meals for street animals from day one, rising to 20% once the a-Paw apps together cover their running costs for three months in a row.

Built as a feature inside Care-a-Paw first, then released as its own app using the same engine.
