Snap‑a‑Paw

Turn your pet into a work of art.

Vision

Every pet gets its moment as art, and every portrait helps a street animal eat.

Mission

Beautiful AI portraits of pets in seconds, as images people share and prints they keep, with a share of every sale funding meals for street animals.

Why it exists

Impulse and gift income: fast to try, easy to share, so it also brings new people into the a-Paw family.

Who it’s for: Pet owners who love sharing their pet, people buying gifts, and people remembering a pet that has died.

What it does

Built around real days on the street and at home

  • Upload 1 to 3 photos, auto crop to the pet
  • Styles: oil, watercolour, royal, cartoon, pop art, seasonal
  • Memorial style, gentle wording, for pets that have passed
  • Save, share, set as wallpaper
  • Order a print (opens website shop)
  • Moderation: pets only, no people or unsafe images

Getting started

  1. Download Snap-a-Paw from Google Play (or try it in your browser) and sign in with your a-Paw account.
  2. Choose a clear photo of one pet facing the camera, or use a photo from Care-a-Paw.
  3. Pick a style and tap Paint my portrait. It takes about a minute. Save it, share it or make it your wallpaper.

Common questions

Is the first portrait free?

Yes, with a small Snap-a-Paw mark. Portrait packs (5 or 15) have no mark and work in every style.

A portrait looks wrong or offensive.

Portraits are painted by AI. Open it and tap Report, and we’ll look at it. If a portrait can’t be made, your credit comes back.

I bought a pack but don’t see it.

Open Packs and tap Restore purchases, and make sure you’re signed in with the same a-Paw account.

How do I change my password or email, or delete my account?

In the app: Account, then Manage account. Or on the web at the a-Paw account page. Deleting removes your account and what you added, in every a-Paw app.

How do I contact you?

In the app: Account, then Contact support. A real person reads every message. Or email support@spot-a-paw.com.
