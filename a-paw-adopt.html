Mission app

Adopt‑a‑Paw

Find the one who's been waiting for you.

Built Both halves work: people browse, keep and apply; rescues put animals up, publish them and read the replies.

Vision

Every street or shelter animal gets a fair chance to be seen and chosen.

Mission

Help rescuers and shelters show their animals beautifully and help adopters find the right match, with half of all income going to feed the animals still waiting.

Why it exists

Mission first: it turns animals helped through Spot a Paw and OneTailOneMeal into adoptions, and earns a modest income from shelter plans.

Who it’s for: Adopters and fosterers; small rescues, shelters and individual rescuers (including in Egypt).

What it does

Built around real days on the street and at home

  • Browse by species, age, size, location, good with kids/cats
  • Animal profiles with photos, story, health, rescuer
  • Apply to adopt or foster, with a short questionnaire
  • Messaging between adopter and rescuer
  • Rescuer dashboard: listings, applications, adopted
  • Happy tails: adoption stories
Technology and AI

Where AI helps in Adopt‑a‑Paw

Every line says whether it works today, is coming soon, or is planned.

  • PlannedSuggest animals that fit an adopter's home and routine, and explain why, so the first meeting is a good one.
  • PlannedDraft an animal's profile and story from its rescue record, for the rescuer to edit.
  • PlannedTranslate profiles and messages between Arabic and English.
Free

What everyone gets

  • Adopters: browse, save, apply, message
  • Rescuers and shelters: up to 5 active listings
Paid

What costs money

  • Shelter Pro A$9.99/month: unlimited listings, featured animals, application inbox, adoption forms
  • Featured listing A$4.99 for 7 days (rescuers)

Prices are planned and may change before launch.

How it helps street animals

Adopters never pay. Shelters can choose a paid plan, and 50% of Adopt-a-Paw's net revenue funds meals for street animals.

Animals rescued through Spot a Paw can be listed in one tap. New adopters are invited to Care-a-Paw.
