Six roles,
one team

  • FeederFeeds street animals and logs each feeding spot.
  • DriverDrives rescued animals and donated meat to where they need to go.
  • RescuerAnswers reports of animals that are hurt or in danger.
  • VetTreats rescued animals and offers advice.
  • ShelterTakes in animals that need a safe place to stay.
  • ButcherOffers spare meat and bones for collection.

What the app
does for you

  • AI route creation and optimisationBuilds the shortest feeding round through the spots that need food.
  • Create your own routePick your spots and save the round you walk or drive.
  • Feeding spotsSee which spots were fed today and which still need food.
  • Butcher meat offersButchers post spare meat, and feeders claim it for collection.
  • CommunitiesVets, butchers, rescuers and feeders, all in one place.
  • Photo-assisted reportsSnap a photo and the app helps fill in what kind of paw and what it needs.
  • Rescue statusNeeds help, help underway, rescued. Everyone sees where each paw is up to.
  • Vets and shelters nearbyClinics and shelters within your radius, one tap away.
