Origin app

Spot‑a‑Paw

So no animal is passed by twice.

GET IT ONGoogle PlayDownload on theApp Store
Visit spot-a-paw.com
Vision

Every street animal in trouble is seen, and somebody nearby knows about it.

Mission

Let anyone photograph a street animal that needs help and put it on a map, so the people nearby — feeders, drivers, rescuers, vets, shelters and butchers — can sort it out between them.

Why it exists

The first app and the community it all rests on. It earns nothing and is not meant to: the feeding routes, the volunteer roles and the colony history live here, and every other a-Paw app borrows from it.

Who it’s for: People in Cairo and other cities who walk past animals in need, and the volunteer community that answers — feeders, drivers, rescuers, vets, shelters and butchers.

What it does

Built around real days on the street and at home

  • Report an animal: photo, AI-suggested species, how many, what it needs
  • Map and feed of animals waiting for help nearby
  • Help this Paw: say you are going, then comments between the people who turned up
  • Mark rescued, with what happened
  • Feeding routes and spots kept by the community
  • Butcher boards: who is donating meat and where to collect it
  • Six roles with karma: feeder, driver, rescuer, vet, shelter, butcher
  • Moderation queue, because reports are public
Technology and AI

Where AI helps in Spot‑a‑Paw

Every line says whether it works today, is coming soon, or is planned.

  • TodayTurns a photo into a draft report: the animal and its visible needs. You check and correct it before posting.
  • TodayBefore a report goes up, looks for what may be the same animal reported nearby in the last three days.
  • TodaySearch in your own words: finds related reports even when the wording differs.
  • TodaySuggests feeding rounds from recorded feeding patterns, highlighting colonies that may be overdue.
  • TodayArabic and English side by side: community updates are translated next to the original.
  • TodayDrafts a shareable rescue story from a report's timeline, for you to review.

How it helps street animals

Spot a Paw is free and always will be. It earns nothing: the paid a-Paw apps pay for it.

Feed-a-Paw takes its feeding spots and volunteer roles from here, and its route concept: a Feed-a-Paw round is a route of spots in order, adopted for the truck drivers. Adopt-a-Paw can list an animal rescued here in one tap. Track-a-Paw reuses the same map and community.

Getting started

  1. Download Spot a Paw and create a free account with your email, Google, Facebook or Apple.
  2. Allow location if you like: the map then opens where you are. It works without it too.
  3. See an animal that needs help? Tap +, add a photo, drop the pin and send. People nearby see it.

Common questions

Is my exact location shown?

No. Reports show the spot of the animal, never where you live, and sensitive feeding spots can be shared only with verified caretakers.

Someone is being unkind or posting junk. What can I do?

Every report and profile has Report and Block in its menu. We read every report.

Does it cost anything?

No. Spot a Paw is free, has no ads, and never sells your data.
