Mission app

Feed‑a‑Paw

One tail. One meal. Every day.

GET IT ONGoogle PlayDownload on theApp Store
Try it in your browserAdd it to your phone
Vision

No street animal goes hungry where OneTailOneMeal is at work, starting with the truck.

Mission

Show the feeding work as it happens, organise the people who make it possible (feeders, butchers, drivers), and make it easy for supporters to fund meals.

Why it exists

The home of OneTailOneMeal: where supporters see every meal, follow the truck, and give, and where the team runs the feeding rounds.

Who it’s for: Supporters around the world, volunteer feeders, butchers who donate meat, drivers, and the truck team.

Inside the app

What Feed‑a‑Paw does today

Everything here is built and working. Screens follow.

Built and working
  • A round follows a route: the driver picks one and the places appear in the order they are driven
  • Every stop logged with meals, animals seen and a photograph
  • Tonight in pictures: the photographs from the round, as they are taken
  • Finishing a round shows what it came to — meals, stops, animals seen
  • Stories from the street, with the pictures that go with them
  • Look around without an account; nothing in the app asks for money
  • Look & feel: seven regional skins, from No Frills to Africa
What it does

Built around real days on the street and at home

  • Live meals counter and truck fund progress
  • Feeding map: spots, rounds done today (locations blurred for safety)
  • Truck tracker (when the truck runs)
  • Driver rounds follow a route: an ordered list of feeding spots, the route concept adopted from Spot a Paw and made for the truck drivers
  • Volunteer tools: log a feed, meat pickup and drop-off (from Spot a Paw's feeder/butcher/driver roles)
  • Stories and photos from the street
  • Support button that opens the website
Technology and AI

Where AI helps in Feed‑a‑Paw

Every line says whether it works today, is coming soon, or is planned.

  • Coming soonA live count of meals served, from the round the truck team logs.
  • PlannedPlan truck routes and portions from Spot a Paw's feeding records, so meals go where they are most needed.
Free

What everyone gets

  • The whole app is free (store rules: apps that raise money for a cause must be free)
  • Follow feeding rounds, meals counter, stories
  • Volunteers log feeds, pickups and deliveries
Money

What it sells

  • No in-app payments
  • Give on onetailonemeal.com or GoFundMe (opens the browser): one-off or monthly
  • Sponsors: local businesses sponsor a route or a week of meals (arranged off-app)

Prices are planned and may change before launch.

How it helps street animals

Feed-a-Paw sells nothing. Gifts made on this website go to the One Tail One Meal operation: the truck, the food and the people who drive it.

Feeding spots and roles come from Spot a Paw. The meals-funded totals from every a-Paw app show here.

Common questions

How do I change my password or email, or delete my account?

In the app: Account, then Manage account. Or on the web at the a-Paw account page. Deleting removes your account and what you added, in every a-Paw app.

How do I contact you?

In the app: Account, then Contact support. A real person reads every message. Or email support@spot-a-paw.com.
