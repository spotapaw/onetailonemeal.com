Track‑a‑Paw

Lost pet? Your neighbours are looking too.

In review With Google Play for review; try it in your browser now. Inside: alerts, a live map, sightings, possible matches, shareable alert pages, private messages, report and block, and QR collar tags. iPhone follows.

Try it in your browserAdd it to your phone
Vision

No pet stays lost for long, because the whole neighbourhood can help bring it home.

Mission

Help lost pets get home fast with instant local alerts, smart QR tags and sightings, and put part of every plan and tag towards feeding street animals.

Why it exists

A trusted safety app every pet owner keeps installed, earning from protection plans and QR tags while growing the community that also powers Spot a Paw.

Who it’s for: All pet owners (prevention), anyone who finds a pet, local groups, vets and shelters.

Inside the app

Real screens from Track‑a‑Paw

Shown with a sample dog, Biscuit. Every screen can wear one of seven regional skins.

Built and working
  • Possible matches: post a found dog and see lost dogs within 10 km straight away
  • Share a link: anyone can open the alert in a browser and send a sighting, no app needed
  • Every sighting on a map, numbered, so you can see which way your pet is moving
  • Areas I watch: home, work or the park
  • Pets home again nearby: the reunions around you
  • Report and block, so the community stays kind
What it does

Built around real days on the street and at home

  • One-tap Lost my pet: photo, last seen place, alert out
  • Map of lost, found and sighted pets nearby
  • Sighting reports with photo and location
  • QR tag page: pet photo, contact owner, medical notes
  • Found a pet: scan tag or post found
  • Reunited stories
Technology and AI

Free

What everyone gets

  • Register pets with photo and microchip
  • Post a lost or found alert (small radius)
  • See nearby alerts and report sightings
  • Printable QR poster
Paid

What costs money

  • Protect plan A$3.99/month or A$29.99/year
  • Wider alert radius and instant push to everyone nearby
  • Boosted alert (one-off A$9.99) for 72 hours
  • Smart QR tag A$14.95 (sold on the website): anyone who scans it can contact you without seeing your number
  • Found-pet chat that hides your phone number

Prices are planned and may change before launch.

How it helps street animals

10% of Track-a-Paw's net revenue funds meals for street animals from day one, rising to 20% once the a-Paw apps together cover their running costs for three months in a row.
