Everything about your pet, in one place.

Get it on Google PlayTry it in your browser
Vision

Every pet has a complete, shared record of its health and life, and every owner who cares for one helps a street animal too.

Mission

Make caring for a pet simple and shared: health records, reminders and memories in one app, with part of every subscription feeding street animals.

Why it exists

The main cash-flow app. Pet owners already pay for organisation and peace of mind; Care-a-Paw turns that into steady monthly income for the OneTailOneMeal truck.

Who it’s for: Dog and cat owners in Australia, the US and the UK first. Families and couples who share care. People who use sitters, kennels or dog walkers.

Getting started

  1. Download Care-a-Paw from Google Play (or try it in your browser) and create your free a-Paw account.
  2. Add your pet: a photo, name, kind and birthday. One pet is free; Premium adds more.
  3. Add records and reminders, such as vaccinations, flea treatment and tablets, and share the pet with family or a sitter.

Common questions

Will I get reminded?

Reminders show in the app and on your home screen today, and you can add any reminder to your phone’s calendar with one tap. Phone notifications are coming.

Can my partner or sitter see my pet?

Yes. Open the pet and tap Share. Choose whether they can add and edit records or only look.

What does Premium add?

More pets, unlimited reminders, document storage and three portraits a month. You can cancel any time in Google Play.

My pet is lost. What do I do?

Open the pet and tap Lost?. It posts an alert on Track-a-Paw with your pet’s photo, so neighbours can help.

How do I change my password or email, or delete my account?

In the app: Account, then Manage account. Or on the web at the a-Paw account page. Deleting removes your account and what you added, in every a-Paw app.

How do I contact you?

In the app: Account, then Contact support. A real person reads every message. Or email support@spot-a-paw.com.
