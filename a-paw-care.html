Earner app

Care‑a‑Paw

Everything about your pet, in one place.

Ready for launch Built and ready for Google Play: pets, health records, reminders, sharing, the vet summary, the story, birthdays and a one-tap lost alert. iPhone follows.

Vision

Every pet has a complete, shared record of its health and life, and every owner who cares for one helps a street animal too.

Mission

Make caring for a pet simple and shared: health records, reminders and memories in one app, with part of every subscription feeding street animals.

Why it exists

The main cash-flow app. Pet owners already pay for organisation and peace of mind; Care-a-Paw turns that into steady monthly income for the OneTailOneMeal truck.

Who it’s for: Dog and cat owners in Australia, the US and the UK first. Families and couples who share care. People who use sitters, kennels or dog walkers.

Inside the app

Real screens from Care‑a‑Paw

Shown with a sample dog, Biscuit. Every screen can wear one of seven regional skins.

Built and working
  • Lost? One tap posts an alert on Track-a-Paw with your pet's photo, so neighbours can help
  • Reminders go into your phone's own calendar too, repeat included
  • Birthdays on the home screen, with a painted portrait to mark the day
  • A weight chart that shows the change at a glance
  • Look & feel: seven regional skins, from No Frills to Africa
What it does

Built around real days on the street and at home

  • Pet profiles with photo, breed, birthday, microchip, vet
  • Health timeline: vaccinations, vet visits, medications, weight, grooming
  • Smart reminders (before vaccines are due, monthly tablets, feeding)
  • Family sharing: invite people, assign tasks ("Sam will do it")
  • Document storage and a vet summary PDF
  • Story tab: milestones and photos
  • Portraits (Snap-a-Paw inside the app)
  • "Meals funded" counter

Free

What everyone gets

  • 1 pet
  • Vaccinations, vet visits, medications, weight
  • Up to 3 reminders
  • 1 trial portrait (with logo)
Paid

What costs money

  • Premium A$6.99/month or A$49.99/year, 7-day free trial
  • Unlimited pets and reminders
  • Share with family and pet sitters
  • Store documents (vaccination certificates, microchip, insurance)
  • Health summary PDF for the vet or kennel
  • 3 Snap-a-Paw portraits a month, no logo

Prices are planned and may change before launch.

How it helps street animals

10% of Care-a-Paw's net revenue funds meals for street animals from day one, rising to 20% once the a-Paw apps together cover their running costs for three months in a row.

Portraits come from Snap-a-Paw. Lost pet button hands over to Track-a-Paw. Sitters can see records through Mind-a-Paw (owner's permission).
