Earner app

Mind‑a‑Paw

Run your pet-minding business from your phone.

In development Today's round, a visit written up for the owner, and the client list. Bookings and invoices are being added.

Vision

Every pet sitter, walker and groomer runs a calm, professional business, and every owner knows exactly how their pet's day went.

Mission

Give pet minders simple tools for bookings, clients and visit reports, and let owners follow along for free, with part of every plan feeding street animals.

Why it exists

Higher-value business subscriptions (fewer customers, more per customer) that also feed owners into Care-a-Paw.

Who it’s for: Independent pet sitters, dog walkers, groomers, small kennels. Owners use it free to follow visits.

What it does

Built around real days on the street and at home

  • Client and pet list (can import from the owner's Care-a-Paw with permission)
  • Calendar and bookings, repeat walks
  • Visit report: check-in, photos, notes, walk map, fed/meds done
  • Invoices and payment reminders
  • Owner app view: live visit updates
  • Care notes: allergies, medications, vet, emergency contact
Technology and AI

Where AI helps in Mind‑a‑Paw

Every line says whether it works today, is coming soon, or is planned.

  • PlannedTurn a minder's quick notes and photos into a tidy visit report for the owner.
  • PlannedSuggest the day's visit order to cut travel time between clients.
Free

What everyone gets

  • Minder: up to 5 clients, visit reports
  • Owner: see visit reports and photos, message the minder
Paid

What costs money

  • Pro A$19.99/month or A$199/year: unlimited clients, scheduling, invoices, reminders to clients
  • Team A$39.99/month: several staff, shared calendar
  • Later: take card payments for bookings (small fee)

Prices are planned and may change before launch.

How it helps street animals

10% of Mind-a-Paw's net revenue funds meals for street animals from day one, rising to 20% once the a-Paw apps together cover their running costs for three months in a row.

Pet records flow from Care-a-Paw (owner's permission). Minders can post lost-pet alerts to Track-a-Paw.
